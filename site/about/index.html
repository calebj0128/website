ABOUT ME

A little about me.

Social work, human connection, and the questions that shape how we live.

I am currently pursuing my Master of Social Work at Wayne State University after earning my bachelor’s degree in Psychology from the University of Michigan.I chose to pursue social work because of my belief in the importance of human connection, empathy, and supporting people through life’s challenges. I am passionate about helping individuals recognize their strengths, find meaning in their experiences, and work through barriers that may be impacting their lives. I was also drawn to social work because of the versatility of the field and the many different ways it allows me to support individuals, families, and communities.I am interested in working with a variety of populations, including young men, veterans, high school and college students, individuals experiencing substance use concerns, and those navigating grief and trauma. My goal is to meet clients where they are in their journey and support them in identifying meaningful ways to improve their well-being and overall quality of life.Outside of social work, I enjoy creating and listening to music, writing, and cycling. I also have a strong interest in philosophy and spirituality and enjoy exploring questions related to meaning, purpose, and the human experience. When appropriate and aligned with a client’s own values and interests, I hope to thoughtfully incorporate these perspectives into my work.
